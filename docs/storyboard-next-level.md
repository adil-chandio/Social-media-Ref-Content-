## Technical lock

- **Master:** 9:16 vertical, 720 × 1280, 30 fps; reference runtime ~34.05 sec, about 1,022 frame slots (final frame count to be conformed to source audio/video timestamps).
- **Audio:** keep the supplied reference audio stream untouched; all visual accents are timed around it, with no new music or SFX layered on top. The render is now cue-mapped to analyzed source transients: scene cuts land on nearby attacks, the greeting uses ~267 ms character steps, skill focus advances every ~800 ms, card/orbit motion follows the measured pulse, and the closing mark hits the 30.391/30.890 sec accents. The long audio pause is treated as an intentional quiet hold.
- **Palette:** blueprint cobalt `#2373A8`, warm paper `#F0EEE7`, near-black `#111215`, high-contrast white, Monarch amber `#EF965B` as the one recurring signal.
- **Type:** Manrope for large words and IBM Plex Mono for micro-labels; avoid tiny essential copy and keep mobile safe zones clear.
- **Silhouette:** use Adil’s supplied AI silhouette PNG with its checkerboard removed to real transparency. Keep it one connected, solid-black shape—no face, clothing detail, glow on the body, or visible source background.
- **Motion language:** every reveal has a readable start, focal action, and settle. Use a recurring amber runner across cuts, eased position/scale, controlled overshoot, type masks, graphic match-cuts, orbital motion, card sheen/parallax, and short easing overlaps—not random shake, excessive glow, or effects that obscure the reference’s clean design.
- **Output checks:** inspect the rendered video at every second and at all cut points; check text legibility, silhouette edges, aspect ratio, audio packet identity, and phone-safe composition.

## Story arc

**Signal → identity → capability → proof → signature.** The amber dot is the visual thread: type cursor, orbit marker, selected skill, portfolio highlight, then the final Monarch/Adil lockup.

## What makes the next pass more premium

1. **Silhouette integration:** use the supplied AI silhouette as a true alpha cutout, preserve its wide-shoulder/long-torso proportions, and animate the entire figure as one shape.
2. **A single visual motif:** the amber point migrates through the sequence, connecting cursor → orbit → skill focus → card detail → logo.
3. **Varied pace:** fast role cuts, a more spacious orbital phrase, a continuous capability conveyor, then a short proof/card burst and calm signature ending.
4. **Real hierarchy:** large readable identity and proof; microtype is supporting detail only.
5. **Premium compositing:** controlled paper/ink/cobalt transitions, soft card shadow, subtle parallax, clean antialiasing, and intentional hold frames.
6. **No unsupported personal claims:** use only Adil’s stated roles and profile figures already present in the source profile; omit extra handles or metrics unless confirmed.
