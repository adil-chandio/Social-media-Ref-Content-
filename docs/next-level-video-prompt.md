# Master Prompt — Adil Chandio / Monarch Portfolio Intro

> Use this as the creative brief for the next edit or AI-assisted motion-design pass. The supplied reference is a guide to pacing and broad choreography—not a source for another creator's identity, watermark, or exact graphic assets.

## Prompt

Create a **premium, personality-rich, frame-animated vertical portfolio intro for Adil Chandio**, using the supplied ~34-second reference video as the timing and pacing guide. The result should feel like a custom motion-design piece from a confident creative studio: cinematic but graphic, playful but precise, tactile but minimal. It must feel authored for Adil and Monarch—not like a stock template, slideshow, generic AI reel, or a collection of unrelated effects.

### Core creative idea

Build one coherent visual story around **SIGNAL → IDENTITY → CAPABILITY → PROOF → SIGNATURE**. A small Monarch-amber signal travels through the entire film: it starts as the greeting's type cursor, becomes the role-card underline, circles through the orbital scene, selects skills, scans the proof panels, catches the light on the identity card, then resolves into the closing signature. Let every transition have a visual reason: a line becomes an orbit, an orbit point becomes a selection marker, a card edge becomes a title rule. Maintain clear, deliberate pacing, with a few joyful moments of kinetic typography and satisfying graphic transformations.

### Visual identity

- **Canvas:** 9:16 vertical, 720 × 1280, 30 fps; conform the finished video to the source's exact duration.
- **Palette:** warm paper `#F0EEE7`, near-black `#111215`, cobalt blue `#2373A8`, clean white, and Monarch amber `#EF965B` as the single recurring accent. Keep color disciplined; no arbitrary rainbow gradients.
- **Typography:** Manrope for display type; IBM Plex Mono for tiny technical labels. Keep the important copy large, high-contrast, and comfortably inside mobile safe areas. Do not use illegible microtext as decoration.
- **Materials:** subtle printed-paper grain, precise blueprint grids, clean vector strokes, restrained soft shadows, dimensional stacked paper/card layers, and controlled specular highlights. Texture should be barely perceptible, never noisy.
- **Composition:** use generous negative space and a strong hierarchy. Keep Adil's name, silhouette, and approved claims uncluttered and readable on a phone.

### Story and motion direction

1. **Opening signal (0–2 sec):** Start on a near-black design grid. Draw guides with confident timing; type “HELLO” on character by character, with the amber cursor following the final glyph. Let the cursor become a punctuation dot, then use it to trigger a clean warm-paper transition. The opening should feel like a designed interface waking up—not a generic glitch intro.
2. **Identity reveal (2–5 sec):** Reveal “I’m Adil Chandio” with a composed type mask. Introduce the supplied transparent, pure-black silhouette as one intact shape, easing into position with natural scale and a subtle settling motion. Preserve its aspect ratio and recognizable silhouette; no facial detail, clothing detail, fake photorealism, glow on the body, checkerboard, or cropping across the head/shoulders.
3. **Role-card rhythm (5–8 sec):** Create short, sharply timed cards for “AI LEARNER” and “CONTENT CREATOR,” followed by a warm-paper “Adil Chandio” card. Make the amber rule or cursor physically motivate each cut. Use crisp snap-to-grid motion and small, controlled overshoots—never random shaking.
4. **Playful creative manifesto (8–15 sec):** Move into a confident cobalt-blue motion playground. Keep multiple orbital paths moving at different speeds, with a few distinct markers and bespoke, readable vector tool icons. Animate the phrases “Creativity is like a game” and “and yes, we like to play” as layered kinetic type: mask reveals, opposing-direction entrances, brief scale contrast, and a satisfying shared-baseline settle. Maintain lively motion throughout this section; do not leave a static text-only hold.
5. **Capability carousel (15–22 sec):** Turn the amber marker into a selector for a continuous, looping, eight-item skills conveyor. Keep several neighboring skills visible while the selected skill grows brighter/larger and the others remain legible. Use the following truthful labels: **AI Learning; AI Content Creation; YouTube Automation; Workflow Automation; Software Development; Monarch Agent Builds; Creator Tools & Systems; Motion & Video Editing.** Animate the active counter/progress ticks in sync. Do not show only two options or let the list disappear between loops.
6. **Proof montage (22–23 sec):** Reveal the approved proof panels in a confident, staggered sequence: “6M+ VIEWS,” “4+ CHANNELS,” “AI × CREATIVITY,” and “MONARCH / BUILDS.” Count figures up smoothly, draw a rule across each panel, and give panels a subtle offset-paper shadow. Do not invent, inflate, or add metrics; retain the figures only if still approved by Adil.
7. **Dimensional identity card (23–24 sec):** Make a custom warm-orange identity card feel tactile and dimensional using a small, controlled tilt, soft cast shadow, restrained highlight sweep, and paper layers that drift by just a few pixels. Keep the printed name and role fully readable. The movement should feel premium and physical—not like a spinning 3D template.
8. **Orbit to signature (24–31 sec):** Bring back the orbital motif, with amber points traveling on separate paths before the geometry resolves around Adil's name. Transition to a quiet near-black grid. Let “Adil” and “Chandio” enter from opposite sides and lock into one perfectly centered wordmark; reveal “AI LEARNING • CREATOR • DEVELOPER” once, then hold it cleanly. Use fine alignment lines and barely moving grid detail; the name must remain the hero.
9. **Close (31–34 sec):** Resolve to a minimal, elegant Adil/Monarch signature. Draw or reveal the mark cleanly, give the amber accent one final restrained pulse, and fade smoothly to near-black while respecting the source audio tail. Do not add a handle, CTA, claim, or third-party logo that Adil has not approved.

### Audio and edit rules

Use the supplied source clip's audio **exactly as provided**. Do not regenerate, replace, remix, time-stretch, denoise, or add music/SFX. ### Quality bar / reject if

- Any scene feels frozen where the direction calls for continuous motion, especially 9–15 sec or 15–22 sec.
- Text is mistyped, clipped, too small for a phone, or outside safe margins.
- A transition is decorative but has no relationship to the amber signal or the visual element before/after it.
- There is gratuitous glow, glitch, shake, stock footage, random particles, unreadable labels, or a generic AI montage look.
- The silhouette's proportions are altered, its alpha edge looks dirty, or any checkerboard/background remains.
- Any unapproved personal claim, fabricated stat, reference-creator identity, watermark, handle, or logo appears.
- Audio differs from the supplied source, the frame rate/aspect ratio is wrong, or the video ends abruptly.

Before delivery, inspect the opening, every scene transition, all text-heavy moments, the silhouette edges, the card close-up, the final signature, and the final frame.
